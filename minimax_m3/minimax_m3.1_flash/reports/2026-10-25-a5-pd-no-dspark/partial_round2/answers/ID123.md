Now, 50 seconds pass in the ejecta frame. We need the corresponding time in the Galaxy frame: Δt = γ Δτ, where γ = 1/sqrt(1 - v²/c²).

v = 60,000 km/s = 0.2c (c = 300,000 km/s). v²/c² = 0.04. γ = 1/sqrt(0.96) = 1.02062...

Δt = 50 × 1.02062 = 51.031 s.

Distance in Galaxy frame: L = v Δt = 60,000 × 51.031 = 3,061,861 km ≈ 3,060,000 km.
