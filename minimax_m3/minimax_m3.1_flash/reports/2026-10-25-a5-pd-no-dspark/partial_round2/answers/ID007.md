For the process γγ → e⁺e⁻ to occur, the invariant mass squared of the two-photon system must exceed the threshold value s = (2mₑ)².

**Kinematics:** For a γ-ray of energy E₁ colliding with a background photon of energy E₂ at angle θ between them:

$$s = 2E_1E_2(1-\cos\theta)$$

The most effective (and dominant) collisions are **head-on** (θ = 180°), which gives:

$$s = 4E_1E_2$$

**Threshold condition:** At threshold, the electron and positron are produced at rest in the CM frame:

$$s_{th} = (2m_e c^2)^2 = 4m_e^2c^4$$

Setting these equal:

$$E_1E_2 = m_e^2c^4 \quad\Longrightarrow\quad E_1 = \frac{(m_ec^2)^2}{E_2}$$
