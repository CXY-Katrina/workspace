The Interaction between the bosonic field X and fermions is given by

\mathcal{L}\left(y\right)=-\lambda{f}\bar{\psi}{f}\left(y\right)\left(\upsilon+X\left(y\right)\right)\psi_{f}\left(y\right)

## 模型完整原始回答

```text
The boson X has mass 6 GeV. It couples to fermions with coupling λ. The decays allowed are to fermion-antifermion pairs whose masses satisfy m_f < m_X/2 = 3 GeV.

Fermions with mass less than 3 GeV: up, down, strange, charm (charm ~1.27 GeV), muon (0.105), electron (0.0005), tau (1.777 GeV). Top quark mass ~173 GeV — too heavy. Bottom quark mass ~4.18 GeV — too heavy (need 2*4.18 = 8.36 > 6).

So allowed: c, s, u, d quarks, and tau, mu, e leptons. That's option B.

Option A includes b b̄ which requires 8.4 GeV > 6 GeV — not allowed. Option D includes t t̄ — not allowed. Option C excludes muons — but muons are lighter than tau, so muon pairs are allowed. So B.

Answer: B.</think>**Kinematic requirement:** For a boson of mass $m_X = 6$ GeV to decay into a fermion–antifermion pair, the two-body decay must satisfy

$$m_f + m_{\bar f} \le m_X \quad\Longrightarrow\quad 2m_f \le 6\ \text{GeV} \quad\Longrightarrow\quad m_f \le 3\ \text{GeV}.$$

**Checking each fermion:**
- $t$: $m_t \approx 173$ GeV → kinematically forbidden ($2m_t \gg 6$ GeV)
- $b$: $m_b \approx 4.2$ GeV → forbidden ($2m_b \approx 8.4$ GeV $> 6$ GeV)
- $c$: $m_c \approx 1.27$ GeV → allowed
- $s$: $m_s \approx 0.1$ GeV → allowed
- $u,d$: light → allowed
- $\tau$: $m_\tau \approx 1.78$ GeV → allowed
- $\mu$: $m_\mu \approx 0.106$ GeV → allowed
- $e$: $m_e \approx 0.0005$ GeV → allowed

Since the coupling $\lambda f\bar\psi_f (y)(\upsilon + X(y))\psi_f (y)$ is universal in flavour, all these light fermions couple to $X$.

**Conclusion:** The allowed lowest-order decays are $X\to c\bar c,\, s\bar s,\, u\bar u,\, d\bar d,\, \tau^+\tau^-,\, \mu^+\mu^-,\, e^+e^-$. The $b\bar b$ mode (A) is excluded because $2m_b > m_X$, the $t\bar t$ mode (D) is excluded, and (C) wrongly omits the muon pair.

Answer: B
```
